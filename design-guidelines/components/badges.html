Usage Rules

VariantMeaning
SuccessPositive state: Paid, Present, Active, Grade A
WarningCaution needed: Late, Partial, Pending, Grade C
DangerProblem state: Absent, Overdue, Failed, Suspended
InfoNeutral information: Leave, In Review, Submitted
PrimaryBranded categories: class name, group, tag
NeutralInactive or unset states: Inactive, Waived, N/A
