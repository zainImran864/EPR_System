Alerts

Inline banners for page-level feedback. Always placed above the relevant content section, not inside modals.

Variants — with title

✅
Attendance saved
Today's attendance for Class 10-A has been recorded successfully.
⚠️
Fee deadline approaching
3 students have unpaid fees due by June 5, 2026.
❌
Failed to save marks
An error occurred while submitting exam results. Please try again.
ℹ️
New term starting
Summer term begins July 1, 2026. Class assignments are open for review.

Usage Rules

VariantUse when
SuccessAn action completed: saved, submitted, imported, exported.
WarningSomething needs attention but is not a blocker: pending fees, low attendance.
DangerAn error occurred that blocked the action. Always explain what to do next.
InfoNeutral context: new feature, upcoming event, available download.
